3 The readings

A reading is written when you ask for it, by Google’s Gemini, from the cards you drew, your question and your zodiac sign. When Gemini cannot be reached, the app composes the reading on your phone from the cards’ own meanings.

Take a reading for what it is: something to think with. It does not foretell anything. It is not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and it should not be the reason for a decision about your health, your money or another person. An AI can write things that are wrong or unsuitable. If a reading troubles you, “Report this reading” sends it to us.

You may share a reading or a card with people you know. Do not sell them or present them as your own professional service. The app’s design, artwork and code belong to the publisher.
