AI Mystic (the “App”) is an AI tarot and astrology app, published by an independent developer (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

AI-generated content. Readings, horoscopes, and astrology content in this App are generated by an AI model and are provided strictly for entertainment. They are not predictions of the future and are not professional, medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice. You can report objectionable AI content from within the App, or by emailing apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

IV

4. Ads

The App may show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Ads are personalised only where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt before serving personalised ads. You can disable ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) or remove ads by subscribing.

V

5. Children

The App is intended for users aged 17 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

VI

6. Data retention & deletion

Inputs are sent to the Gemini API to produce a reading and are not retained by us afterwards. Your saved readings and settings live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.

VII

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information.

Network requests use HTTPS. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly — we never see your payment method.
