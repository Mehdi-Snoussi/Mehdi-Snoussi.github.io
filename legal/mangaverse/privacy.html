Privacy Policy

MangaVerse — Effective 4 June 2026. Last updated 4 June 2026.

1. Who we are

MangaVerse (the “App”) is a manga discovery and tracking app, published by an independent developer (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

2. Data we collect

CategoryWhat it isWhyWhere it lives
Library & trackingThe titles you follow, your reading status, and lists.To sync and restore your tracking list.Stored via Google Firebase, tied to a randomized installation identifier — not to your name or email.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, SMS messages, calendar, location, microphone audio, or photos. The App has no sign-up or login.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing — processes the “Remove Ads” purchase. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads (only if you have not removed them). AdMob & ads privacy
  • Google Firebase — stores your tracking list under a randomized identifier. Firebase privacy

4. Ads

The App may show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Ads are personalised only where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt before serving personalised ads. You can disable ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) or remove ads via in-app purchase.

5. Children

The App is intended for teen and older audiences and is not directed at younger children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Data retention & deletion

On-device data is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Your tracking list stored via Firebase is tied to a randomized installation identifier; to request its deletion, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com and we will action verified requests within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information.

8. Security

Network requests use HTTPS. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly — we never see your payment method.

9. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.
