Privacy Policy

MathCam — Effective 4 June 2026. Last updated 4 June 2026.

1. Who we are

MathCam (the “App”) is a math problem solver, published by an independent developer (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

2. Data we collect

CategoryWhat it isWhyWhere it lives

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, SMS messages, calendar, location, or microphone audio. The App has no sign-up or login.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Gemini API — processes the photo you submit to solve the problem. Gemini API terms
  • Google Play Billing — processes purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads (only if you have not subscribed). AdMob & ads privacy

4. Ads

The App may show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Ads are personalised only where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt before serving personalised ads. You can disable ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) or remove ads by subscribing.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience including students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Data retention & deletion

Photos are sent to the Gemini API to produce a solution and are not retained by us afterwards. Your settings and recent solutions live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information.

8. Security

Network requests use HTTPS. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly — we never see your payment method.

9. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.
