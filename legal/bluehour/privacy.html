PP·01 · Identification

1. Who we are

Blue Hour (the “App”) is a home screen for car displays and for phones in a dashboard mount. It shows a scene lit by the real sun and weather where you are, with your music, your next turn and your apps on top. It is published by Carthage Studio (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

PP·03 · Services

3. Third-party services

  • MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) — provides the weather from a rounded position sent over HTTPS. Their terms: api.met.no terms of service. Weather data is licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional “Blue Hour Full” purchase. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment method. Google Privacy Policy

When you open another app from the dock or the drawer, that app's own privacy policy applies.

PP·04 · Position

4. Location

The App asks for location while it is in use. It does not use background location. If you decline, the scene is lit for the main city of your time zone instead, and drive mode switches only when you tap it.

You can change this at any time in your device settings.

PP·05 · Notifications

5. Notification access

Notification access is optional and is turned on by you in your device's system settings. With it, the App reads the media sessions of your music apps and the navigation notifications of apps such as your map app, so it can show what is playing and your next turn, and send play, pause and skip to your player. It does not read, store or send any other notification content.

The App never takes audio focus, so it does not interrupt your player or your radio. You can turn notification access off at any time in your device settings.

PP·06 · Sharing

6. Sharing a scene

If you tap Share, the App renders an image of the scene on your device and hands it to your system share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to us. Where it then goes is governed by the app you choose to share into.

PP·07 · Audience

7. Children

The App is made for drivers and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

PP·08 · Retention

8. Data retention & deletion

Your settings and records live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Weather requests are not stored by us, and we hold no server-side copy of anything, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.
