TS·04 · Accuracy

4. Directions, speed and weather

The next turn shown in the App is copied from your navigation app's own notification, the speed comes from your device's GPS, and the weather comes from MET Norway. Any of them can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. They are shown for convenience only: always follow the road, its signs and your navigation app, and never rely on the App for safety-critical decisions.

The scene's light is computed from the sun's position and is an illustration, not a measurement of road or visibility conditions.
