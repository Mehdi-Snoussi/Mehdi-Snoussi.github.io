At a glance

Every point here is restated in full in the sections below.

  • No account needed. Only the optional Friends and Duo ask for a Google sign-in.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • Everything you log stays on your phone, and on your own watch if you add Mini Me there. We keep no copy of it.
  • Our only server is for Friends and Duo. It holds what your friends see (the name you choose, your friend code and your mini’s look), your friends, the statuses you share, visits and your duo. Never your steps, sleep, water, cycle, check-ins or coins.
  • Friends are added only with a code and a yes. Minis talk in preset phrases only: no chat, no photos, no free text.
  • Delete my Friends account in Settings removes your Friends data from our server at any time.
  • Steps and sleep are read from Health Connect only if you allow it. They are shown on your phone, today’s steps also on your own watch, and never sent to us or shared. Your drinks are written to Health Connect only if you switch that on.
  • Your cycle log never appears on the widget, over your apps, on your watch or in a notification.
  • Google backup is used only when it is end-to-end encrypted, and you can turn it off.
  • Mini Me is for people aged 13 and over.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Who we are

Mini Me (the “App”) is a tiny you that lives on your phone: you dress your mini, look after it, and it walks over your apps, sits on your home screen and reacts to your real day. It is published by Carthage Studio (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect, the App asks Android's Health Connect for read access to two kinds of data: Steps and Sleep. It reads your steps for the last 7 days and last night's sleep when you open My day or your mini's trip, and again when you come back to them. It uses them only to show your day and to unlock your mini's trip on an active day or after a good night.

If you turn on Save to Health Connect on the Water screen, the App also asks for write access to Hydration. It then writes each drink you log there (how much, and when), and removes it again if you undo it. It writes nothing else, reads no hydration data, and asks for nothing else.

The data is read on your phone and held in memory while it is shown. It is not stored on your phone, not included in backups or in your export file, and never sent to us or to anyone else. No person reads it.

If you use Mini Me on a Wear OS watch (section 8), today’s step count is also sent to that watch, your own device, so its tile and watch face can show it. The watch keeps only today’s count, until the next one replaces it. Your sleep never goes to the watch.

Mini Me's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to anyone (today’s steps go only to your own watch, as above), and never used to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

You can turn any of these permissions off at any time in Health Connect's settings, or from Settings → Health Connect in the App (and Save to Health Connect on the Water screen). The App keeps working without them.

4

Your cycle log

The cycle log is optional and is off until you switch it on. What you log stays in the App's private storage on your phone. The App calls these days “cosy days” everywhere outside the cycle screen: cycle words never appear on the home-screen widget, on the mini that walks over your apps, on your watch, or in a notification. Your watch is told only whether today is a cosy day, so your mini can show its cosy look there; nothing from your cycle log goes to it. The two cycle reminders (packing your kit, and logging the last day) say only “Time to pack your kit” and “Cosy days over?”.

Lock Cycle asks for your phone’s own lock (fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern) before the cycle screen opens. Android checks it; the App never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN, and stores only that the lock is on.

Your cycle log is included in your Google backup only when that backup is end-to-end encrypted (section 6), and in an export file only when you make one yourself. Delete everything in Settings removes it from your phone.

On your phone: permissions and features

  • Notifications — reminders, at most one a day, plus water’s sip reminders if you choose them, are planned and scheduled on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn each one off in Reminders (sips also on the Water screen).
  • Fingerprint or screen lock — used only if you turn on Lock Cycle, to open the cycle screen. Android checks it; the App never sees it.
  • Display over other apps — lets your mini walk over your apps when you switch it on. The App only draws your mini; it does not see, read or record what other apps show. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android's settings.
  • Home-screen widgets — pictures of your mini drawn on your phone.
  • Start after a restart — puts your reminders and the walking mini back after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration — small taps when you play with your mini.

Backups and your export file

Google backup. Android can back up the App's data to your Google account. The App allows this only when the backup is end-to-end encrypted with your phone's screen lock (Android 9 and later), so neither Google nor we can read it. It contains your mini, your logs and your settings, including your cycle log, but never health data from Health Connect. Mini Me on your watch is never backed up: your phone sends it everything again. You can turn it off in Settings → Back up to Google. Google stores and deletes Android backups under its own terms.

Export file. Export my data writes a file named mini-me-backup.json to your phone's Downloads folder, only when you tap it. The file holds your mini, your logs and your settings, including your cycle log, and it is not encrypted: anyone who has the file can read it, so keep it somewhere safe. Restore from a file checks a file before it replaces anything.

WhatsApp stickers

The App draws twelve sticker pictures of your mini on your phone. When you tap Add to WhatsApp, WhatsApp on the same phone receives those pictures with the pack's name and our contact and policy links. When your mini's look changes, the pack is redrawn so WhatsApp can update it. Nothing else is shared with WhatsApp. Once a sticker is in WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies, including to stickers you send in chats.

Mini Me on your watch

Mini Me has an optional app for Wear OS watches, with a tile, complications and a watch face. If you install it on a watch paired with your phone, your phone sends it what it needs to show your mini: your mini’s pictures, needs, coins and level; today’s mood and streak; your status (never words you wrote for it); your trips, workouts, focus sessions and Iftar times if you use them; whether today is a cosy day; and today’s step count. The watch shows them in the language you picked in the App.

What you do on the watch — a check-in, a status, a workout, a focus session, playing with your mini — goes back to your phone, which saves it as if you had done it there.

All of this travels only between your own phone and watch, over the Wear OS connection that Google Play services provides, and never reaches us. The watch keeps only the latest copy. Delete everything on your phone clears it from the watch too. The Mini Me watch face holds no data of its own: it shows what the watch app gives it.

Friends and Duo

Friends and Duo are optional, and the rest of the App works without them and without an account. They are the only part of the App that uses a server: Google’s Firebase, set up by us for Mini Me.

Signing in. Friends asks you to sign in with your Google account, so each Friends account belongs to a real person. Firebase Authentication then keeps your Google email address, name and profile picture link, a Firebase user ID, and when you signed up and last signed in. It also logs IP addresses for a few weeks to stop abuse, and it processes all of this in the United States. Your friends never see your email, your Google name or your picture.

What our Friends server keeps (Cloud Firestore, stored in the European Union):

  • Your profile: the name you choose (letters only, up to 16), your friend code, whether your mini is Mimi or Kai, your mini’s look, and when Mini Me was last open. You and your friends see it.
  • Friend requests: when you type someone’s code, they receive a request with your name, code and mini. Nobody can search or list people: a request needs the exact code, and you become friends only when the other person says yes.
  • Your friends and since when, your close friends and the people you block. Only you see your close friends and blocks.
  • Statuses you share: the preset status, whether it is for all your friends or your close friends, and until when. Statuses set to Only me, and words you write for a status, never leave your phone.
  • Visits: which of six preset phrases your mini brought, and when. The friend you visited sees it until they put it away.
  • Your duo, if you have one: the two of you, since when, the last hug and snack, and the Together bar. Only the two of you see it.
  • Reports: when you tap Report, who sent it, about whom, and the last preset phrase that person sent you. Only we read reports.

Never on the server: your steps, sleep, water, cycle log, check-ins, moods, workouts, coins, room or anything else you log. Nobody can type messages to anyone: minis talk only in preset phrases.

On your phone. The App keeps a copy of what your friends share with you (their names, codes, minis and statuses) in its private storage, so the Friends tab still works offline. It is never put in your Google backup or your export file. With a duo, your home-screen widget shows your duo friend’s name and mini.

The real app only. With its requests to the server, the App sends a Google Play Integrity check, through Firebase App Check, so that our server can turn away requests that do not come from the real Mini Me. Firebase uses this check only to protect the server.

Signing out and deleting. Sign out (Settings → Friends account) keeps your Friends data on the server for when you come back. Delete my Friends account deletes your profile, friend code, requests, friendships, close friends, blocks, statuses, the visits you sent and received, your duo, and your Firebase account with its Google email; Google may ask you to sign in again first. Delete everything does the same. Google then removes the account from Firebase Authentication’s backups within 180 days. A report is not removed with an account: we delete each report once we have looked at it, and within 90 days at the latest. If you uninstall Mini Me without deleting your Friends account, email us and we will delete it (how to ask).

Who else. Google runs Firebase for us as our service provider, under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. We, the Developer, can open the server’s data in the Firebase console, and we do so only to look at a report, to answer a request from you or to fix a problem. Friends data is never sold, never shared with anyone else and never used for advertising.

Children and teens

Mini Me is for people aged 13 and over. The App asks for an age group before it starts, and it does not open for anyone who picks “under 13”. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. The App has no ads and no chat. Friends are built to be safe for teens: friends only with a code and a yes, preset phrases instead of free text, no photos, and Block and Report on every friend.

Data retention & deletion

Your data lives on your phone until you remove it. Delete everything in Settings erases your mini, coins, check-ins, cycle log and every other record at once, clears your watch too and deletes your Friends account; uninstalling the App or clearing its data does the same on your phone, and uninstalling Mini Me from your watch removes everything it kept. An export file you made stays where you put it until you delete it.

Our server holds only Friends and Duo data, and keeps it until you delete your Friends account in the App or ask us to (section 9). We hold no server-side copy of anything else. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, correct, delete and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw any permission at any time from your phone's settings or Health Connect. What stays on your phone you can see in the App, export and delete yourself. Your Friends data is shown in the App: you can change your name there, and Delete my Friends account removes it; for a copy of what our server holds about you, email us. We use Friends data only to provide Friends and Duo, which you chose to use. We do not sell personal information.

Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read, on your phone and on your watch. What your watch shows travels only over the Wear OS connection between your own devices. Google backups are end-to-end encrypted. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly. An export file is not encrypted (section 6).

The Friends server accepts only Google sign-ins and checks every read and write against strict rules: only your friends can read your profile and the statuses you share with them, only you can change or delete your own records, and anything else is refused. Friends data travels over HTTPS, and Google encrypts it where it is stored.

If the App ever runs into an error, it notes the last few in a small file in its own folder on your phone, so a problem can be traced on the phone it happened on. That file holds the App's error messages, not your entries, is never sent anywhere, and goes when you uninstall the App. If the App cannot open its saved data, it offers to keep a copy of that file in your Downloads, for you to keep.
