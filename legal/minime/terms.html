Mini Me is a game. Nothing in it is medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment, and it is not a medical device.

The App does not require an account. Everything you log lives on your phone, and what your watch shows lives on your watch if you use Mini Me there. Your Friends account (your name for friends, friend code, friends and duo) lives on our server, so it comes back when you sign in on a new phone; your mini and everything else you log do not. You can sign out or delete your Friends account at any time in Settings.
