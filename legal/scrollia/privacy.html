Privacy Policy

Scrollia — Effective 4 June 2026. Last updated 4 June 2026.

1. Who we are

Scrollia (the “App”) is a fiction-reading app (original Romance and LitRPG novels), published by an independent developer (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, SMS messages, calendar, location, microphone audio, or photos. The App has no sign-up or login.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing — processes purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads in free chapters (only if you have not removed them). AdMob & ads privacy
  • Content delivery network — delivers chapter files; requests include your IP address but do not identify you.

4. Ads

Free chapters may show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Rewarded ads (to unlock a chapter) are always optional. Ads are personalised only where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt before serving personalised ads. You can disable ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) or remove ads via in-app purchase. The reading screen itself is kept free of banner ads.

5. Children

The App is intended for users aged 17 and over. Some novels contain mature themes. The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Data retention & deletion

Your reading state and settings live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or use Settings → Reset App Data. We do not hold a server-side copy of your personal content. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information.

8. Security

Chapter files are delivered over HTTPS and stored on your device in encrypted form. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly — we never see your payment method.

9. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.
