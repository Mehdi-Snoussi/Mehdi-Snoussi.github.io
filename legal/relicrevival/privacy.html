PP.2026.01Identification

1. Who we are

Relic Revival (the “App”) is a relaxing restoration game in which you wipe grime from procedurally generated antiques, uncover what they really are, and build a museum. It is published by Carthage Studio (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

PP.2026.04Display & consent

4. Ads and your consent

The App may show interstitial ads (only after you finish appraising a relic, never during a wipe) and rewarded ads (only when you choose to watch one — for example to double an appraisal, or to double your offline earnings). It shows no banner ads and no ads on launch or exit.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states where required, we show a consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the App. You can also disable ad personalisation at the OS level (Android: Settings → Google → Ads), or remove forced ads via the in-app purchase.

PP.2026.05Reminders

5. Notifications

The App can show an optional local daily reminder. Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device — no data leaves it, and there is no push server and no device token. You can turn them off at any time in the App's settings or your device settings.

PP.2026.06Reproduction

6. Sharing a relic

If you tap Share, the App renders an image on your device and hands it to your system share sheet. Nothing is uploaded to us. Where it then goes is governed by the app you choose to share into.

PP.2026.07Audience

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are requested with settings appropriate for a general audience.

PP.2026.08Retention

8. Data retention & deletion

Your progress and settings live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. We hold no server-side copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.

PP.2026.09Rights

9. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Privacy options). We do not sell personal information.

PP.2026.10Conservation

10. Security

Network requests use HTTPS. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly — we never see your payment method.
