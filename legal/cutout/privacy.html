CutOut — Effective 4 June 2026. Last updated 4 June 2026.

CutOut (the “App”) is a background remover, published by an independent developer (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

3. Third-party services

  • Google ML Kit — performs background removal on your device. No image is uploaded for this. ML Kit terms
  • Google Play Billing — processes purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads (only if you have not subscribed). AdMob & ads privacy

4. Ads

The App may show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Ads are personalised only where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt before serving personalised ads. You can disable ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) or remove ads by subscribing.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Data retention & deletion

Your photos and settings live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. We do not hold a server-side copy of your content. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information.

8. Security

Image processing happens on your device. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly — we never see your payment method.

9. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
