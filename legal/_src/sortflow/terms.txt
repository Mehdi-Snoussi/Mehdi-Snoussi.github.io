SortFlow has no sign-up. Your levels, coins and settings are stored on your phone. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, they are gone, and we have no copy to give back. Android's own backup may return them on a new phone, and "Export progress" in Settings lets you keep a file yourself. Purchases come back through "Restore Purchases".

Coins and power-ups, whether earned or bought, exist only inside the game. They have no value in money, cannot be sold, exchanged or refunded on their own, and are lost if the game's data is cleared.

Refunds follow [Google Play's refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). We can support a request you send to Google, and Google decides. Where you live in the EU or the UK, the rights the law gives you over digital purchases are untouched by these terms.
