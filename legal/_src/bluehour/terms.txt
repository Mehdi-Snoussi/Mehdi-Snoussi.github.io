## Driving comes first

You are the driver, and driving safely is your responsibility. Choose scenes, dress the car, type in the car book and change settings only while the vehicle is parked. ## The turn, the speed and the weather

- The next turn is copied from your navigation app's own notification.
- The speed, and everything the car book works out from it, comes from your device's GPS.
- The weather comes from MET Norway.

Any of these can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. They are there for convenience. Follow the road, its signs and your navigation app, and never rely on Blue Hour for a decision that affects safety. The speed on screen is not your speedometer and is no defence against a fine.

The light in the scene is worked out from the sun's position. It is a picture, not a measurement of visibility or of the state of the road. The car book's consumption and cost figures are arithmetic on what you typed, not an official record.
