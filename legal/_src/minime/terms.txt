Mini Me is a game. Nothing in it is medical advice, a diagnosis or a treatment, and it is not a medical device.

- **Cycle log.** The cosy days it shows are rough estimates from the days you logged. Never use them for birth control, or to plan or avoid a pregnancy. If something about your cycle worries you, talk to a doctor.

- **Ramadan mode.** Suhoor and Iftar are calculated on your phone for the city you pick. They can differ from your mosque or local authority by a few minutes. Follow your local times.
