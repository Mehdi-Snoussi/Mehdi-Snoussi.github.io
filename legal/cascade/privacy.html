Privacy Policy

Cascade — Effective 18 June 2026. Last updated 18 June 2026.

1. Who we are

Cascade (the “App”) is a drop-and-merge puzzle game, published by an independent developer (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”). Contact: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.

This policy explains, in plain language, what data the App handles and why.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, SMS messages, calendar, location, microphone audio, or photos. The App has no sign-up or login and works offline apart from serving ads and processing purchases.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing — processes the “Remove Ads” purchase. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — shows ads (only if you have not removed them). AdMob & ads privacy

4. Ads

The App may show banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads via Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always optional and shown only when you choose to watch one (for example, to claim a free power-up or double a run's coins). Ads are personalised only where you allow it. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we show a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before serving personalised ads. You can disable ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads) or remove ads via in-app purchase.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience. It is not specifically directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

6. Data retention & deletion

Your game progress and settings live on your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data. We do not hold a server-side copy. For any request, email apps.contact.supports@gmail.com; we action verified requests within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the EU/UK (GDPR) or California (CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, delete, and object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information.

8. Security

Network requests use HTTPS. Purchases are validated by Google Play directly — we never see your payment method.

9. Changes

If we materially change this policy we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: apps.contact.supports@gmail.com.
