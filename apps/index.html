Carthage Studio

Seven Android apps, made in Tunisia by Mehdi Snoussi. Each one is free on Google Play.

  1. Dhikr

    Tasbih, azkar and Quran

    Tasbih counter, azkar, prayer times with the adhan, the qibla and the Quran, offline.

    Install Dhikr on Google Play

  2. SortFlow

    Color sort puzzle

    Pour colors from tube to tube until each holds one. A calm water sort, offline.

    Install SortFlow on Google Play

  3. AI Mystic

    Tarot card reading

    Pull a daily tarot card and get an AI tarot reading written for your sign.

    Install AI Mystic on Google Play

  4. Cascade

    Drop and merge puzzle

    Drop orbs, merge two alike and chain big cascades. A calm merge puzzle, offline.

    Install Cascade on Google Play

  5. Zellia

    Tile match palace

    Tap three tiles alike to clear them and restore a palace of zellige. No timer.

    Install Zellia on Google Play

  6. Relic Revival

    Clean and restore

    Wipe the grime off old relics and find out what you found. A calm restore game.

    Install Relic Revival on Google Play

  7. Blue Hour

    Car screen launcher

    Car launcher with a living scene, lit by the real sun and weather. No ads.

    Install Blue Hour on Google Play
